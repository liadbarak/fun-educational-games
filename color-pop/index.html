Color Pop

A little aim. A lovely chain reaction.

A colorful challenge, one shot at a time

Color Pop is an original color matching game from PuzzleTen. Aim your launcher, bounce shots off the walls and connect a group of matching bubbles. This free online bubble game starts with a simple goal: clear 30 bubbles.

How to play Color Pop

  1. Move your mouse to aim, then click to fire. On touch screens, hold and move to aim, then release. The dotted guide shows your path, including wall bounces.
  2. Connect three or more bubbles of the same color to pop the entire matching group. This bubble matching game combines match-3 color puzzles with careful aiming.
  3. Cut a group off from the ceiling and its floating bubbles fall for bonus points. Popped and dropped bubbles both count toward your goal.
  4. Reach the goal to unlock the next level. After five non-matching shots, a new row arrives. If the bubbles reach the danger line before you finish, retry the level.

Make every connection count

Each popped bubble earns 10 points; each disconnected bubble earns 20. Consecutive matching shots add a combo bonus: 15 extra points on your second success, 30 on your third, and so on. A non-matching shot ends the combo. Check the next bubble preview before choosing your angle.

Later levels raise the goal and introduce deeper, staggered layouts. Five colors become six from level 4. Each color has its own symbol to help distinguish it. Clear the whole board and you also complete the level, even if you have not reached the target.

Play your way

Use the aim buttons and Pop! button, or focus the board and use Left/Right arrows to aim and Enter/Space to fire. Reduced-motion preferences simplify the effects. Restart level resets only the current level's points and progress; previous level points remain. Your run lasts until you leave or reload this page.
