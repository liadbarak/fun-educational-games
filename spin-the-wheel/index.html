SPIN THE WHEEL

Add your choices, press SPIN, and let the wheel make the pick.

FREE RANDOM WHEEL PICKER

This simple wheel spinner makes a random choice from any list. Use it as a random name picker for a classroom, choose what to eat, settle a group decision, pick an activity, or turn a list of games into your next move. Change the sample choices above or paste one option per line, then spin the wheel as many times as you like.

HOW TO USE THE RANDOM WHEEL

  1. Edit the sample options, remove the ones you do not need, or add more.
  2. To add a whole list quickly, open Paste a list and put one choice on each line.
  3. Press SPIN. The pointer shows the randomly selected result.
  4. Spin again, or remove the winner before the next spin so it cannot be picked twice.

WHAT CAN I PUT ON THE WHEEL?

Anything you can type: names, numbers, foods, chores, exercises, movie choices, team members, quiz topics or yes-or-no decisions. The random picker runs in your browser and is ready immediately—there is no account, login or setup screen.

QUESTIONS

Is the wheel really random?
Yes. A winner is selected randomly on every spin, then the wheel animates to that result. Every option has the same chance of being selected.

Can I paste names from a spreadsheet?
Yes. Copy a column, open Paste a list, and paste it. Blank lines are ignored and each remaining line becomes one wheel segment.

Does the wheel save or send my list?
No. Your choices stay on this page in your browser and disappear when you leave or refresh it.
