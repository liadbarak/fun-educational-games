Play a Dress Up Game Online

Build a look from hair to shoes in PuzzleTen’s free dress up game. Explore 70 original illustrated items, including tops, skirts, trousers, dresses, jackets and accessories. Tap a piece to see it on your character immediately. There is no download, signup or timer: start with a fashion challenge, or switch to Free Dress Up and follow your own ideas.

How to Play

  1. Choose your mode. Fashion Challenge gives you a theme; Free Dress Up lets you create without a score.
  2. Build your outfit. Choose a category, then tap an item to wear it. Pick shoes and either a dress or a top and bottom. Add hair, layers and accessories to finish the details.
  3. Finish your look. Select Rate My Look in a challenge, or Finish My Look in free mode.
  4. Explore your result. Challenge scores explain theme, color, accessory and style matches.
  5. Keep creating. Try the theme again with another combination or select Next Challenge for a different prompt.

Dresses replace tops and bottoms, and selecting a top or bottom removes a dress. Layers can go over either. You can wear a head accessory, a necklace or scarf, and a bag together. Remove items with the button below the wardrobe. A neutral base outfit always stays on the character.

Fashion Challenge

Style a 90s party outfit, experiment with Y2K shapes, or dress for a beach day. The 15 themes also include goth, prom, fairy, street style, winter, rainbow and space-inspired looks. The theme stays above the character as you browse, and item labels offer clues about their style.

Scores are based on what you wear, not chance. Theme Match is worth 40 points, Color Match 25, Accessories 20, and Style Bonus 15. Theme and style points reward matching item tags; color points reward the theme’s palette. Two matching accessories can earn full accessory points. Skin tone never affects your score. Different combinations can do well, and the same outfit always receives the same score for the same theme.

The score is a playful measure of the challenge brief, not a judgement of your taste. In Free Dress Up, there is no score at all.

Create Your Own Outfit

Use Free Dress Up as a relaxed outfit maker: try a soft blouse with wide denim, layer a jacket over a dress, or mix unexpected colors. This online outfit game is a space to experiment with an original illustrated wardrobe, without needing to match a theme. Finish your look to see it cleanly, then create another.

Today's Fashion Challenge

Everyone gets the same daily theme, changing at midnight UTC. Complete it to save your best score for that day in this browser. You can retry as often as you like. Local storage is optional: the game still works if saving is unavailable. Scores do not sync between devices, and clearing site data removes saved results.

Dress Up Game FAQ

Is this dress up game free?

Yes. All 70 wardrobe items, Free Dress Up and Fashion Challenge are free to use, with no account required.

Can I play the dress up game online without downloading anything?

Yes. Play directly in your browser. Saving an image of your finished look is optional and only happens when you choose to save or share it.

How does Fashion Challenge work?

You receive a theme and choose an outfit. The game compares the selected items’ style tags, colors and accessories with that theme, then gives a score out of 100. There is more than one way to score well.

Can I create my own outfit?

Yes. Free Dress Up lets you mix hair, clothing, shoes and accessories without a scoring requirement. Choose Finish My Look when you are happy with it.

What fashion styles are available?

The wardrobe includes casual, sporty, vintage, elegant, streetwear, goth, cute, fairy-inspired, Y2K and 90s pieces, along with seasonal and party looks. All illustrations are original PuzzleTen designs.

Can I play on mobile?

Yes. Tap a category and an item to dress the character. Scroll within the wardrobe to browse more items while your character remains beside it. On a keyboard, use Tab to move between buttons and Enter or Space to select.

Is there a new fashion challenge every day?

The daily challenge rotates through the 15 themes at midnight UTC. Your best result for the current day is saved locally when your browser allows it. Use Next Challenge for more themes without waiting.

Can I share my outfit?

After finishing, select Share My Look to prepare a picture with your outfit, theme and challenge score. Supported browsers offer native sharing; otherwise, use Save My Look to download a PNG. No image is uploaded automatically.
