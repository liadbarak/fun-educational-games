For parents This game exercises number bonds to 10 — the pairs of numbers that add up to ten, which is one of the first things children are asked to recall instantly rather than count out. Here they aren't tested on it; spotting the pairs is the only way to play, so the recall gets practised naturally. The five pairs stay listed beside the board as a prompt for anyone still learning them.
