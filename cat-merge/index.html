Cat Merge

Merge cute kittens, discover new cats and complete your Cat Collection in this free online merge game.

How to Play Cat Merge

  1. Find a pair. Tap a cat, then an identical cat directly above, below, left or right of it. The pair becomes the next evolution. You can also drag one onto the other.
  2. Make room. Move a cat to any empty space to bring a pair together. Every successful move or merge brings one new cat into the garden, except every third merge: that earns a free space with no new arrival. The next arrival is shown above the board.
  3. Keep growing. Merging earns points; moving alone does not. A full garden is still playable if two matching neighbors remain. The round ends when there are no empty spaces and no neighboring pairs, or when you discover the final cat.

On a keyboard, Tab to a space and press Enter or Space to select it. Use the arrow keys to move between spaces, then Enter or Space to choose a destination. Escape clears your selection. There is no timer, so take your time.

Discover Every Cat

Twelve original personalities live in this cat evolution game. A Tiny Kitten becomes a Playful Kitten, then a Curious Cat. Beyond the cozy Fluffy Cat are adventurers, spellcasters and some much less ordinary residents. The final evolution stays a surprise until you find it.

Each first discovery adds a portrait to your Cat Collection. Open the collection below the board to see familiar faces and the silhouettes still waiting to be revealed. Later arrivals become higher-level cats as you reach new evolutions in the current round, helping you build toward your next discovery.

Cat Merge Tips

  • Merge before moving. A merge frees a space for the next arrival. Moving into an empty space uses up room when the new cat appears.
  • Check your neighbors. Diagonal cats cannot merge. Choose a destination that leaves the new evolution near another cat of the same level.
  • Watch the next arrival. It may create a useful pair. Its landing space is chosen by the game, so leave several options open.
  • Protect your empty spaces. Keep high-level cats near the edges when possible, leaving the center available for connecting smaller pairs.

About Cat Merge

Cat Merge is an original, free browser game from PuzzleTen. This cute cat game uses a small garden and neighboring matches instead of falling objects or physics. Play on desktop, tablet or phone without downloading anything or creating an account.

Your best score, discoveries and current garden save in this browser when local storage is available. They do not sync between devices, and clearing site data removes them. Sound is optional, and the short effects respect your device’s reduced-motion preference.

Cat Merge FAQ

What is Cat Merge?

Cat Merge is a single-player puzzle game where matching cats evolve into new characters. Build your collection while keeping enough room in a four-by-four garden.

How do you play Cat Merge?

Combine two identical cats in neighboring spaces by tapping or dragging. Move cats into empty spaces to set up pairs. A new cat arrives after each move or merge, with a no-arrival break every third merge.

How many cats can you discover?

There are 12 distinct evolutions, starting with Tiny Kitten and ending with a secret final cat. Discovered portraits remain in your collection between rounds.

Is Cat Merge free?

Yes. You can play this free merge game online without a signup, subscription or download.

Can I play Cat Merge on mobile?

Yes. Tap a cat and then its destination, or drag with your finger. The board fits small screens, and the collection stays collapsed until you open it.

Does my Cat Collection save?

Yes, when browser storage is available. Your collection and best score survive a restart, and your current round resumes after reloading. Private browsing or clearing site data may remove your progress.
