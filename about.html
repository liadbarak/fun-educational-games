ABOUT

Classic games are timeless — simple rules, endless challenge, and genuinely good for your brain. I built this as a love letter to the games I grew up with. Hope you enjoy it.

Everything here is free to play, runs entirely in your browser, and needs no download or signup. If you'd like to support the project, you can buy me a coffee.

PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: August 31, 2026

THE SHORT VERSION

This site does not ask for your name, email, or any personal details, and there is no account to create. The only data collected is anonymous traffic statistics.

WHAT IS COLLECTED

This site uses Google Analytics to understand how many people visit and which games they play. Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser and may collect:

  • Pages you visited and how long you stayed
  • Approximate location (country or city level)
  • Browser, device type, and operating system
  • The site or search that referred you here

This information is aggregated and anonymous. It is not used to identify you individually, and it is not sold or shared with anyone.

YOUR GAME DATA

Your high scores are saved in your own browser's local storage and never leave your device. Clearing your browser data will erase them. Nothing about how you play is sent to any server.

THIRD PARTIES

  • Google Analytics — traffic statistics. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Buy Me a Coffee — only if you choose to click the support link. Any payment is handled entirely on their site under their privacy policy. No payment details ever reach this site.

YOUR CHOICES

You can block analytics cookies through your browser settings, or by installing the Google Analytics opt-out add-on. The games work exactly the same either way.

CONTACT

Questions about this policy? Reach out through the support page.
