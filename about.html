ABOUT

Puzzle Ten is a small collection of puzzles and printables, built and run by one person — Liad Barak. There are three games you can play in the browser, twenty-two themed word searches you can play or print with an answer key, and step-by-step drawing guides that each start from a number.

Everything is free, runs entirely in your browser, and needs no download and no signup. The printables are meant to be used: print as many as you like, for a classroom or a kitchen table, and the answer keys come on their own sheet.

It is genuinely all-ages. The word searches have a gentler level that shows what each word means, which suits anyone learning English, and a harder level for people who want a fight. If you'd like to support the project, you can buy me a coffee.

THE SHORT VERSION

This site does not ask for your name, email, or any personal details, and there is no account to create. What is collected is anonymous traffic statistics, plus the cookies that advertising uses.

WHAT IS COLLECTED

This site uses Google Analytics to understand how many people visit and which games they play. Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser and may collect:

  • Pages you visited and how long you stayed
  • Approximate location (country or city level)
  • Browser, device type, and operating system
  • The site or search that referred you here

This information is aggregated and anonymous. It is not used to identify you individually, and it is not sold or shared with anyone.

ADVERTISING

This site uses Google AdSense to show ads, which is how it pays for itself. Google and its partners may set cookies, or read cookies already in your browser, to show ads based on your previous visits to this and other sites.

You can turn off personalised advertising in Google My Ad Center, or opt out of third-party ad cookies at aboutads.info/choices. You will still see ads, they just won't be tailored to you. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before any of this happens, and you can change that answer at any time.

YOUR GAME DATA

Your high scores are saved in your own browser's local storage and never leave your device. Clearing your browser data will erase them. Nothing about how you play is sent to any server.

THIRD PARTIES
