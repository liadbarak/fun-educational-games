ONLINE DICE ROLLER

Use this free dice roller whenever you need a quick random roll without finding physical dice. Choose a die type, select between one and ten dice, and press Roll Dice. Every result appears separately, with the combined total underneath.

ROLL 2 DICE

For a standard two-dice roll, use the Roll 2 Dice preset above. It selects two D6 dice, rolls both at once and adds them together. You can also choose 2 from the number-of-dice menu whenever you want to keep the same setup for several rolls.

D20 ROLLER

A D20 is the twenty-sided die commonly used for checks and actions in tabletop role-playing games. Select D20 from the dice-type menu or press the D20 Roller preset for an instant roll.

SUPPORTED DICE

The roller supports D4, D6, D8, D10, D12, D20 and D100. D6 results use familiar pip faces; the other dice use clear numbered shapes so every result stays easy to read on a phone or computer.

QUESTIONS

Are the dice rolls random?
Yes. Each die is generated independently in your browser, with every face having the same chance of being selected.

Can I roll different kinds of dice together?
Not in this small first version. Every roll uses one selected die type, but you can roll up to ten of that die at once.

Does the dice roller need an account?
No. It works immediately with no login, download or saved configuration.
