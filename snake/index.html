What this exercises Telling food that's still good from food that's finished with — the everyday judgement behind not wasting food, plus route planning as the snake gets longer. Rather than answering a question about it, you carry each item to the fridge or the bin, so the decision is the move you make. Binning good food costs no points; it pauses briefly to say why it could still have been eaten, then play continues.
