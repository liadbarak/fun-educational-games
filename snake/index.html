FOOD RESCUE — A SNAKE GAME WHERE YOU HAVE TO PUT THE FOOD AWAY

Food Rescue is a free browser game built on Snake, with one thing added: picking the food up isn't the point, delivering it is. Anything still good goes in the fridge in the top-left. Anything that's just scraps goes in the bin in the top-right. No download, no signup, and it plays on a phone.

HOW TO PLAY

  • Steer with the arrow keys or the on-screen D-pad. The snake never stops, so you're choosing a route rather than reacting.
  • Drive over an item to pick it up — worth 5 points, and it shows what you're carrying above the board.
  • Carry it to the right corner. Correct delivery scores 25 and the snake grows by one. The correct corner is ringed in green while you're holding something, so you can always check.
  • Get it wrong and the game stops to explain why. You keep the points you had, but you forfeit the 25 — and you still grow and still speed up, so a wrong drop-off is a delivery you paid for and didn't get.
  • Walls and your own body end the run. Your tail tip is the exception — it moves out of the way on the same step, so tight loops are survivable.

WHY THE SORTING ISN'T A QUIZ

Most games that try to teach something interrupt themselves to ask you a question. Here the decision is the move: choosing the fridge or the bin means steering to one corner instead of the other, and there's no way to play without making the call. The only time the game speaks up is when you get one wrong.

Both destinations are fixed in the same corners every run, so the map becomes something you learn rather than something you hunt for. That also means the top of the board is where all the traffic is, which is what eventually kills you — the strategy guide covers the routes that survive, and why the wrong corner counts as a hazard.

THE SORTING ITSELF

Whole food — apples, cheese, milk, carrots, strawberries, broccoli, grapes, tomatoes — goes in the fridge. Peels, bones, wrappers and empty containers go in the bin. No picture ever appears in both lists, so an item always means exactly one thing.

That's a simplification, and a deliberate one: the game is teaching still-good versus finished-with, not refrigeration. A real kitchen is fussier, and tomatoes and bananas and bread are all actively worse for being in a fridge.

QUESTIONS

Is Food Rescue free?
Yes, and there's no signup. Your best score stays in your own browser.

Does it work on a phone?
Yes — the board sits at the top and the D-pad is pinned to the bottom of the screen.

How is it different from ordinary Snake?
In Snake, touching the food is the whole goal. Here it's the start of the errand: you pick an item up, then have to get it to the right corner while getting longer.

Does the game tell me the right answer?
While you're carrying something, the correct destination is outlined in green. The game isn't trying to catch you out — the difficulty is the route, not the recall.

Does the snake grow when I pick food up?
No, only when you drop it off. Collecting costs you nothing in length.

Does it keep getting faster forever?
No. It speeds up for about 24 deliveries and then stops. After that, everything that gets harder is your own length.
