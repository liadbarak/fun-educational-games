BODY PARTS WORD SEARCH

A body parts word search, free to play and free to print with an answer key. It runs from the words a beginner learns first to ones most people have never had to spell.

Body parts are usually among the first fifty words anybody learns in a new language, for the obvious reason that you need them at a doctor's more urgently than you need most vocabulary. The easy level covers exactly that set — hand, foot, head, knee — with a plain definition shown for each one you find.

The harder level is closer to anatomy: COLLARBONE, KNUCKLE, TENDON, PANCREAS, ABDOMEN. Everyone has all of them and almost nobody writes any of them down.

HOW TO PLAY

  • Tap the first letter, then the last letter. No dragging, which matters on a phone where dragging a diagonal across small letters is inaccurate.
  • Across, down or diagonally — never backwards, at either level.
  • Either order works. Tapping the last letter first is fine; it's the line that counts.

PRINT IT

Press Print this puzzle — or just Ctrl‑P — and you'll get the grid exactly as it is on screen, with the word list underneath and a full answer key on a second sheet. Generate a new grid first if you'd like a different one; whatever is on screen is what prints. No account, no watermark, nothing to download.

THE WORDS

  • ARM — the long part between shoulder and hand
  • LEG — you walk on two of these
  • HAND — you hold things with this
  • FOOT — you stand on two of these
  • HEAD — the top of your body, above the neck
  • NOSE — you smell and breathe through this
  • HAIR — it grows on top of your head
  • KNEE — the joint in the middle of your leg
  • MOUTH — you eat and speak with this
  • TOOTH — you chew your food with these
  • HEART — it beats inside your chest
  • FINGER — you have five of these on each hand
