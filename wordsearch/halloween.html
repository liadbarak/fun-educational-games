HALLOWEEN WORD SEARCH

A Halloween word search you can play right here or print for a class or a party. Pick a level, find everything hidden in the grid, and press print when you want it on paper — answer key included.

Halloween is one of the few times of year when a word list writes itself. Everybody already knows what a pumpkin and a broomstick are, which is exactly what makes the theme work at both ends: the easy level uses words a child or a beginner can read at a glance, so the puzzle is about spotting them rather than decoding them.

The harder level reaches for the vocabulary that only shows up once a year — CAULDRON, WEREWOLF, SPECTRE, TOMBSTONE. Familiar enough to picture, awkward enough to hunt for in a 13×13 grid with a clock running.

HOW TO PLAY

  • Tap the first letter, then the last letter. No dragging, which matters on a phone where dragging a diagonal across small letters is inaccurate.
  • Across, down or diagonally — never backwards, at either level.
  • Either order works. Tapping the last letter first is fine; it's the line that counts.

PRINT IT

Press Print this puzzle — or just Ctrl‑P — and you'll get the grid exactly as it is on screen, with the word list underneath and a full answer key on a second sheet. Generate a new grid first if you'd like a different one; whatever is on screen is what prints. No account, no watermark, nothing to download.
