NEW YEAR WORD SEARCH

A New Year word search to play online or print for a party, a classroom, or the long stretch of an evening before midnight. Choose a level, find everything in the grid, and press print for a paper copy with an answer key.

This one works well as a party sheet because the easy level is genuinely easy — year, clock, cake, music, twelve. Nobody has to concentrate hard, which is the point when it is being passed around a room. Every word shows its meaning the moment it is found.

The harder level is a different evening entirely: RESOLUTION, COUNTDOWN, CHAMPAGNE, JUBILANT. Fourteen of those in a 13×13 grid, on the clock, is a real puzzle rather than a warm-up.

HOW TO PLAY

  • Tap the first letter, then the last letter. No dragging, which matters on a phone where dragging a diagonal across small letters is inaccurate.
  • Across, down or diagonally — never backwards, at either level.
  • Either order works. Tapping the last letter first is fine; it's the line that counts.

PRINT IT

Press Print this puzzle — or just Ctrl‑P — and you'll get the grid exactly as it is on screen, with the word list underneath and a full answer key on a second sheet. Generate a new grid first if you'd like a different one; whatever is on screen is what prints. No account, no watermark, nothing to download.

THE WORDS

English learner level — eight of these twelve appear in any one puzzle:

  • YEAR — twelve months, from January to December
  • WISH — something you hope will happen
  • CAKE — a sweet food baked for special days
  • PARTY — a happy gathering with food and music
  • CLOCK — it shows you what time it is
  • HAPPY — the feeling of being pleased
  • DANCE — to move your body in time to music
  • MUSIC — sounds made by singing or instruments
  • NIGHT — the dark hours when most people sleep
  • FRIEND — someone you like and spend time with
  • TWELVE — the number that comes after eleven
  • LIGHTS — lamps that shine in the dark

Native speaker level — fourteen of these are used at a time:

RESOLUTION · MIDNIGHT · CELEBRATE · FIREWORKS · CONFETTI · COUNTDOWN · CHAMPAGNE · STREAMER · TRADITION · CALENDAR · SPARKLER · JUBILANT · JANUARY · FESTIVE · GLITTER · BALLOON · REVELRY · TOAST
