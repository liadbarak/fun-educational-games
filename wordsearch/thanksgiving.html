THANKSGIVING WORD SEARCH

A Thanksgiving word search you can play right here or print for a class, a table of relatives, or the hour before the food is ready. Pick a level, find everything hidden in the grid, and press print when you want it on paper — answer key included.

Thanksgiving is a good word search theme for an awkward reason: the vocabulary is almost entirely concrete. Corn, bread, table, gravy. These are things you can point at, which means the easy level works for a beginner who is still reading slowly — the puzzle is about spotting the word, not decoding it.

The harder level goes after the words that only get said in November — CORNUCOPIA, MAYFLOWER, WISHBONE, DRUMSTICK. Familiar enough that nobody needs a dictionary, long enough to stay hidden in a 13×13 grid with a clock running.

HOW TO PLAY

  • Tap the first letter, then the last letter. No dragging, which matters on a phone where dragging a diagonal across small letters is inaccurate.
  • Across, down or diagonally — never backwards, at either level.
  • Either order works. Tapping the last letter first is fine; it's the line that counts.

PRINT IT

Press Print this puzzle — or just Ctrl‑P — and you'll get the grid exactly as it is on screen, with the word list underneath and a full answer key on a second sheet. Generate a new grid first if you'd like a different one; whatever is on screen is what prints. No account, no watermark, nothing to download.

  • BREAD — a food baked from flour and water
  • APPLE — a round red or green fruit
