BACK TO SCHOOL WORD SEARCH

A back to school word search, free to play here and free to print with an answer key. Useful for the first week of term, a supply lesson, or a settling activity while you take the register.

The easy level is the vocabulary of the room itself — desk, chair, ruler, pencil, crayon. Words a child sees written on things all day, which is precisely why they are good for early reading practice: the puzzle is about recognising a shape you already half know rather than working out a word for the first time.

The harder level is the vocabulary of the system — CURRICULUM, PROTRACTOR, STATIONERY, SEMESTER. Words that live on letters home and timetables, and that a surprising number of adults hesitate over before spelling.

HOW TO PLAY

  • Tap the first letter, then the last letter. No dragging, which matters on a phone where dragging a diagonal across small letters is inaccurate.
  • Across, down or diagonally — never backwards, at either level.
  • Either order works. Tapping the last letter first is fine; it's the line that counts.

PRINT IT

Press Print this puzzle — or just Ctrl‑P — and you'll get the grid exactly as it is on screen, with the word list underneath and a full answer key on a second sheet. Generate a new grid first if you'd like a different one; whatever is on screen is what prints. No account, no watermark, nothing to download.

THE WORDS

English learner level — eight of these twelve appear in any one puzzle:

  • BAG — you carry your things in this
  • BOOK — pages bound together that you read
  • DESK — a table you sit and work at
  • RULER — a straight tool for measuring lines
  • CLASS — a group of people who learn together
  • CHAIR — you sit on this
  • PAPER — thin white sheets you write on
  • LUNCH — the meal you eat in the middle of the day
  • LEARN — find out how to do something new
  • CRAYON — a coloured wax stick for drawing
  • PENCIL — you write with this and can rub it out
  • TEACHER — the person who helps you learn

Native speaker level — fourteen of these are used at a time:

TIMETABLE · STATIONERY · CURRICULUM · ASSEMBLY · HOMEWORK · SATCHEL · CANTEEN · PLAYGROUND · LIBRARY · UNIFORM · PROTRACTOR · CALCULATOR · REGISTER · CORRIDOR · LOCKER · SEMESTER · TEXTBOOK · DETENTION
