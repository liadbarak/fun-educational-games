EASTER WORD SEARCH

An Easter word search to play in the browser or print as a worksheet for a class, a family, or a wet afternoon in April. Choose a level, find everything in the grid, and press print for paper plus an answer key.

Easter is a rare theme where the easy words are also the interesting ones. Egg, chick, lamb, nest, hunt — concrete, visual, and mostly things a beginner has already seen. Each one shows a short plain-English meaning when it is found, so the puzzle teaches while it is being solved.

The harder level runs on the vocabulary of an English spring: DAFFODIL, HYACINTH, MARSHMALLOW, SPRINGTIME. Long words, awkward letters, and a timer — the grid stops being a warm-up and starts being a search.

HOW TO PLAY

  • Tap the first letter, then the last letter. No dragging, which matters on a phone where dragging a diagonal across small letters is inaccurate.
  • Across, down or diagonally — never backwards, at either level.
  • Either order works. Tapping the last letter first is fine; it's the line that counts.

PRINT IT

Press Print this puzzle — or just Ctrl‑P — and you'll get the grid exactly as it is on screen, with the word list underneath and a full answer key on a second sheet. Generate a new grid first if you'd like a different one; whatever is on screen is what prints. No account, no watermark, nothing to download.

THE WORDS

  • LAMB — a baby sheep
  • NEST — the home a bird builds for its eggs
  • HUNT — to search hard for something hidden
  • HIDE — to put something where it cannot be seen
  • BUNNY — another word for a rabbit
  • CHICK — a baby chicken
  • GRASS — the green plants that cover a lawn
  • PAINT — colored liquid you brush onto things
  • CANDY — sweet treats made of sugar
  • BASKET — a container with a handle for carrying things
  • SPRING — the season that follows winter
