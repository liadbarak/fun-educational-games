SPACE WORD SEARCH

A space word search you can play in the browser or print out. Planets, rockets, and everything else above the sky — two levels, a fresh grid every time, and an answer key when you print.

Space is the rare topic where the beginner vocabulary is genuinely useful and genuinely interesting: sun, moon, star, orbit, planet. Short words that carry real meaning, which is why this theme turns up in classrooms far more often than its difficulty would suggest.

The harder level moves out past the solar system — SUPERNOVA, NEBULA, QUASAR, PULSAR. Half the fun there is that most people know roughly what these are without ever having written one down.

HOW TO PLAY

  • Tap the first letter, then the last letter. No dragging, which matters on a phone where dragging a diagonal across small letters is inaccurate.
  • Across, down or diagonally — never backwards, at either level.
  • Either order works. Tapping the last letter first is fine; it's the line that counts.

PRINT IT

Press Print this puzzle — or just Ctrl‑P — and you'll get the grid exactly as it is on screen, with the word list underneath and a full answer key on a second sheet. Generate a new grid first if you'd like a different one; whatever is on screen is what prints. No account, no watermark, nothing to download.

THE WORDS

English learner level — eight of these twelve appear in any one puzzle:

  • SKY — what you see above you outdoors
  • SUN — the star that gives us light and heat
  • MOON — it circles the Earth and glows at night
  • STAR — a burning ball of gas, very far away
  • MARS — the red planet, fourth from the sun
  • EARTH — the planet we live on
  • ORBIT — the path one body takes around another
  • COMET — an icy body with a long bright tail
  • ALIEN — a living thing from another world
  • LUNAR — to do with the moon
  • ROCKET — it launches into the sky on a column of fire
  • PLANET — a large body that circles a star

Native speaker level — fourteen of these are used at a time:

ASTEROID · NEBULA · GALAXY · SATELLITE · ECLIPSE · METEORITE · TELESCOPE · GRAVITY · SUPERNOVA · ASTRONAUT · JUPITER · SATURN · NEPTUNE · URANUS · MERCURY · PULSAR · QUASAR · COSMOS
