OCEAN WORD SEARCH

An ocean word search — sea creatures, the beach, and what lives further down. Play it here or print it with a full answer key on the second sheet.

Almost everything in the easy list is something you could point at: fish, crab, wave, shell, whale. Concrete nouns are the easiest kind of vocabulary to hold in your head while scanning a grid, which is what makes this theme a good first one for anybody still reading word by word.

The harder level goes deeper, in both senses — PLANKTON, NAUTILUS, CRUSTACEAN, ESTUARY, ABYSS. Several of these are words people meet in a documentary and never once write down.

HOW TO PLAY

  • Tap the first letter, then the last letter. No dragging, which matters on a phone where dragging a diagonal across small letters is inaccurate.
  • Across, down or diagonally — never backwards, at either level.
  • Either order works. Tapping the last letter first is fine; it's the line that counts.

PRINT IT

Press Print this puzzle — or just Ctrl‑P — and you'll get the grid exactly as it is on screen, with the word list underneath and a full answer key on a second sheet. Generate a new grid first if you'd like a different one; whatever is on screen is what prints. No account, no watermark, nothing to download.

THE WORDS

English learner level — eight of these twelve appear in any one puzzle:

  • FISH — an animal that swims and has gills
  • CRAB — it walks sideways and has claws
  • WAVE — moving water that rolls onto a beach
  • SAND — tiny grains you walk on at the beach
  • BOAT — it floats and carries people on water
  • SEAL — a smooth grey animal that barks
  • DEEP — a very long way down
  • SHARK — a large fish with rows of sharp teeth
  • WHALE — the biggest animal in the sea
  • SHELL — the hard case a sea creature leaves behind
  • CORAL — a hard, colourful growth under the sea
  • SQUID — a soft creature with ten arms

Native speaker level — fourteen of these are used at a time:
