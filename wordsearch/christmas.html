CHRISTMAS WORD SEARCH

A Christmas word search, free to play here and free to print — grid, word list and a full answer key on a second sheet. Useful for a classroom in December, or for the quiet hour after lunch.

The easy level sticks to the words everyone knows by heart: tree, star, bell, sleigh. That familiarity is the point. When you already know every word on the list, the whole puzzle becomes pattern recognition, which is why this theme suits younger children and beginners better than almost any other.

The harder level goes after the ones that only come out of the cupboard in December — MISTLETOE, YULETIDE, FRANKINCENSE, PANTOMIME. Words most people can define but very few can spell without stopping to think.

HOW TO PLAY

  • Tap the first letter, then the last letter. No dragging, which matters on a phone where dragging a diagonal across small letters is inaccurate.
  • Across, down or diagonally — never backwards, at either level.
  • Either order works. Tapping the last letter first is fine; it's the line that counts.

PRINT IT

Press Print this puzzle — or just Ctrl‑P — and you'll get the grid exactly as it is on screen, with the word list underneath and a full answer key on a second sheet. Generate a new grid first if you'd like a different one; whatever is on screen is what prints. No account, no watermark, nothing to download.

THE WORDS

English learner level — eight of these twelve appear in any one puzzle:

  • ELF — a small helper in a red and green hat
  • TREE — a tall plant people decorate in December
  • GIFT — something you wrap and give to someone
  • SNOW — soft white flakes falling from the sky
  • STAR — it shines at night, and tops the tree
  • BELL — a metal cup that rings when you shake it
  • SANTA — the man in red who brings presents
  • CAROL — a song people sing in December
  • ANGEL — a figure with wings, usually in white
  • SLEIGH — a sledge pulled across the snow
  • CANDLE — wax with a wick that gives light
  • REINDEER — an animal with antlers that pulls a sledge
