SPORTS WORD SEARCH

A sports word search, playable here and printable with an answer key. Two levels that take quite different approaches to the same subject.

The easy level is built from verbs rather than nouns — run, jump, throw, catch, kick. That is deliberate: action words are among the first anyone learns in a new language and among the last to be practised in writing, because they are the words you say rather than read.

The harder level is a list of sports themselves, and leans towards the ones that do not come up every week — LACROSSE, ROUNDERS, DECATHLON, CURLING. You do not need to have played any of them to find them.

HOW TO PLAY

  • Tap the first letter, then the last letter. No dragging, which matters on a phone where dragging a diagonal across small letters is inaccurate.
  • Across, down or diagonally — never backwards, at either level.
  • Either order works. Tapping the last letter first is fine; it's the line that counts.

PRINT IT

Press Print this puzzle — or just Ctrl‑P — and you'll get the grid exactly as it is on screen, with the word list underneath and a full answer key on a second sheet. Generate a new grid first if you'd like a different one; whatever is on screen is what prints. No account, no watermark, nothing to download.

THE WORDS

English learner level — eight of these twelve appear in any one puzzle:

  • RUN — move fast on your feet
  • WIN — finish first, or beat the other side
  • BALL — a round thing you kick or throw
  • SWIM — move yourself through water
  • JUMP — push off the ground into the air
  • TEAM — a group who play on the same side
  • GOAL — where you score a point
  • RACE — a contest to be the fastest
  • KICK — hit something with your foot
  • THROW — send something through the air by hand
  • CATCH — take hold of something in the air
  • MATCH — a game between two sides

Native speaker level — fourteen of these are used at a time:

BADMINTON · GYMNASTICS · ATHLETICS · LACROSSE · ROUNDERS · ARCHERY · FENCING · CANOEING · TRIATHLON · DECATHLON · SNOOKER · CURLING · HANDBALL · ROWING · SAILING · SKATING · HURDLES · DISCUS
