FREE WORD SEARCH — PLAY ONLINE OR PRINT

A word search you play in your browser, or print as a worksheet with an answer key. Every grid is generated fresh, so a theme never runs out, and there is nothing to download or sign up for.

Colours word search The basic dozen, plus the shades English has that nobody actually uses.

TWO LEVELS

English learner uses a 10×10 grid and eight short everyday words, with no timer. When you find a word it tells you what that word means — so the vocabulary is the reward for finding it rather than a test you have to sit.

Native speaker uses a 13×13 grid and fourteen longer, less familiar words, against a clock. Same themes, much less generous.

HOW TO PLAY

  • Tap the first letter of a word, then tap the last letter. No dragging — which matters on a phone, where dragging a diagonal across small letters is fiddly and inaccurate.
  • Words run across, down, or diagonally — never backwards. That holds at both levels, so you never have to read a grid in reverse.
  • Tapping the last letter first is fine too. The line is what matters, not the direction you read it in.

PRINTING

Press Print this puzzle, or Ctrl‑P. You get the grid exactly as it stands on screen, the word list underneath, and a complete answer key on a second sheet. Generate a new grid first if you want a different one — whatever is showing is what prints.

QUESTIONS

Is it free?
Yes, to play and to print, and there's no signup. Your best score stays in your own browser.

Does it work on a phone?
Yes. Tapping two letters is far more accurate on a small screen than dragging across them, which is why the game works that way.

Are the puzzles always the same?
No — each one is generated fresh, so the same theme gives a different grid every time.

Do words ever run backwards?
Never, at either level.

Is this good for learning English?
For vocabulary recognition, yes — the learner level shows a plain meaning for every word you find. It won't teach grammar or pronunciation, and it isn't trying to.
