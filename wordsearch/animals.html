ANIMAL WORD SEARCH

An animal word search you can play here or print out. Pick the level that suits you, find every animal hidden in the grid, and generate a new puzzle whenever you want another — the grid is different every time.

Animals are the theme most people learn first in a new language, and for good reason: they're concrete, most learners already know what the thing is, and the words are short. That makes them a gentle way to practise reading English words as whole shapes rather than sounding out each letter. The English learner level here sticks to the animals a beginner meets early — pets and farm animals, nothing longer than five letters — and tells you what each one means as you find it.

The native level is a different proposition. It draws on animals that are familiar to look at but awkward to spell: PORCUPINE, ALBATROSS, RHINOCEROS. Knowing the animal doesn't help much when you're scanning a 13×13 grid against a clock.

HOW TO PLAY

  • Tap the first letter, then the last letter. No dragging, which matters on a phone where dragging a diagonal across small letters is inaccurate.
  • Across, down or diagonally — never backwards, at either level.
  • Either order works. Tapping the last letter first is fine; it's the line that counts.

PRINT IT

Press Print this puzzle — or just Ctrl‑P — and you'll get the grid exactly as it is on screen, with the word list underneath and a full answer key on a second sheet. Generate a new grid first if you'd like a different one; whatever is on screen is what prints. No account, no watermark, nothing to download.
