SPRING WORD SEARCH

A spring word search you can play online or print as a worksheet. Pick a level, find everything hidden in the grid, and press print when you want it on paper — the answer key prints on its own sheet.

Spring is the most useful theme on this site for a science lesson that is pretending to be a game. The easy level is seed, grow, bud, leaf, nest, rain — which is, more or less, the whole of what a plant does, in words short enough for a beginner to read at a glance.

The harder level makes that explicit: GERMINATE, PERENNIAL, MIGRATION, EQUINOX, POLLEN. Real vocabulary from biology and the calendar, hidden in a 13×13 grid, on a timer.

HOW TO PLAY

  • Tap the first letter, then the last letter. No dragging, which matters on a phone where dragging a diagonal across small letters is inaccurate.
  • Across, down or diagonally — never backwards, at either level.
  • Either order works. Tapping the last letter first is fine; it's the line that counts.

PRINT IT

Press Print this puzzle — or just Ctrl‑P — and you'll get the grid exactly as it is on screen, with the word list underneath and a full answer key on a second sheet. Generate a new grid first if you'd like a different one; whatever is on screen is what prints. No account, no watermark, nothing to download.

THE WORDS

English learner level — eight of these twelve appear in any one puzzle:

  • SUN — the bright star that lights the day
  • BUD — a flower before it opens
  • RAIN — water falling from the clouds
  • SEED — a small thing a new plant grows from
  • GROW — to get bigger over time
  • BIRD — an animal with feathers that can fly
  • NEST — the home a bird builds for its eggs
  • LEAF — a flat green part of a plant
  • WARM — a little bit hot, in a nice way
  • GREEN — the color of grass
  • FLOWER — the colorful part of a plant
  • GARDEN — a piece of ground where plants are grown

Native speaker level — fourteen of these are used at a time:

GERMINATE · GREENHOUSE · PERENNIAL · MIGRATION · BUTTERFLY · BLUEBELL · PRIMROSE · SEEDLING · FRAGRANT · DRIZZLE · EQUINOX · SHOWERS · TADPOLE · SPROUT · MEADOW · POLLEN · NECTAR · BLOOM

QUESTIONS

Is it free?
Yes, to play and to print, with no signup.

Are the puzzles always the same?
No — every grid is generated fresh, so this theme never runs out.

Can I use this in a classroom?
Yes. Print as many as you like. The answer key comes on its own sheet.

Does this work alongside a science topic?
That is what the harder level is for — germination, pollen, migration and the equinox are all in it, so the puzzle doubles as revision.
