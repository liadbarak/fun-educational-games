ST. PATRICK’S DAY WORD SEARCH

A St. Patrick's Day word search you can play right here or print for a classroom in March. Pick a level, find every word in the grid, and press print when you want it on paper with an answer key.

The easy level is short and bright — green, luck, gold, clover, parade, dance. It is a good March sheet for a beginner because almost every word is one syllable and every word is something you can picture, so the difficulty lives in the grid rather than in the reading.

The harder level is more fun than it has any right to be. LEPRECHAUN, SHILLELAGH, BLARNEY, LIMERICK — a set of words that are enjoyable to say, difficult to spell, and genuinely hard to find with a clock running.

HOW TO PLAY

  • Tap the first letter, then the last letter. No dragging, which matters on a phone where dragging a diagonal across small letters is inaccurate.
  • Across, down or diagonally — never backwards, at either level.
  • Either order works. Tapping the last letter first is fine; it's the line that counts.

PRINT IT

Press Print this puzzle — or just Ctrl‑P — and you'll get the grid exactly as it is on screen, with the word list underneath and a full answer key on a second sheet. Generate a new grid first if you'd like a different one; whatever is on screen is what prints. No account, no watermark, nothing to download.

QUESTIONS

Is it free?
Yes, to play and to print, with no signup.

Are the puzzles always the same?
No — every grid is generated fresh, so this theme never runs out.

Can I use this in a classroom?
Yes. Print as many as you like. The answer key comes on its own sheet.

Does this teach anything about Ireland?
A little. The harder level is built from real Irish words and places — Dublin, Blarney, Celtic, limerick — rather than only shamrocks and leprechauns.
