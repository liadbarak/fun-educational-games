FARM WORD SEARCH

A farm word search you can play in the browser or print for a class, with a full answer key on the second sheet.

Farm animals are the most-taught vocabulary set there is, in any language, and for good reason: every word is a thing a child can picture, name and make a noise for. Cow, pig, hen, goat, sheep — all short, all concrete, all learned before almost anything abstract. That makes the easy level here about as gentle as a word search gets.

The harder level is the working vocabulary of actual farming — SILAGE, PADDOCK, IRRIGATION, GRANARY, FURROW. Words that mean something specific to anybody who has done the job and are near-invisible to everybody else.

HOW TO PLAY

  • Tap the first letter, then the last letter. No dragging, which matters on a phone where dragging a diagonal across small letters is inaccurate.
  • Across, down or diagonally — never backwards, at either level.
  • Either order works. Tapping the last letter first is fine; it's the line that counts.

PRINT IT

Press Print this puzzle — or just Ctrl‑P — and you'll get the grid exactly as it is on screen, with the word list underneath and a full answer key on a second sheet. Generate a new grid first if you'd like a different one; whatever is on screen is what prints. No account, no watermark, nothing to download.

THE WORDS

English learner level — eight of these twelve appear in any one puzzle:

  • COW — a large animal that gives us milk
  • PIG — a pink animal that rolls in mud
  • HEN — a female chicken
  • HAY — dried grass fed to animals
  • GOAT — an animal with horns that likes to climb
  • BARN — a big building for animals and feed
  • MILK — a white drink that comes from a cow
  • SHEEP — a farm animal covered in wool
  • HORSE — a large animal you can ride
  • FIELD — open land where crops are grown
  • FARMER — the person who works the land
  • TRACTOR — a machine that pulls heavy tools

Native speaker level — fourteen of these are used at a time:
