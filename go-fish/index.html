Go Fish Online

A little luck. A good memory. One nice catch.

Play Go Fish online for free against Sarah and Alex, two computer opponents. Collect matching cards, remember what other players ask for, and try to finish with the most books. This classic card game works right in your browser on a phone, tablet, or computer, with no download or registration.

How to Play Go Fish

  1. Pick a rank in your hand. Tap or click a card group, such as your 7s. The highlighted border shows your selection.
  2. Choose an opponent. Tap Sarah or Alex to ask for every card they hold of that rank. If they have any, they give them all to you and you ask again.
  3. Go fishing if they have none. Draw one card from the deck. Draw the rank you asked for and you get a “Nice catch!” and another turn. Otherwise, play passes to the next player.
  4. Make books. All four cards of the same rank become a book automatically. Keep collecting until every book is complete.

You can use Tab to move between card groups and opponents, and Enter or Space to select them. A brief first-turn tip guides you through your first request; you can skip it at any time.

How Do You Win?

The player with the most books wins. A book is all four suits of one rank: for example, 7♠, 7♥, 7♦, and 7♣. Each book scores one point, whether it is made of aces, numbers, or face cards. There are 13 books in a standard deck. If players share the highest score, they tie.

Go Fish Rules

  • Cards and players: this version uses one standard 52-card deck without jokers. You and two computer players receive five cards each. The remaining 37 cards form the draw pile. Any books dealt at the start are collected automatically.
  • Asking: ask one opponent for a rank you currently hold. They must hand over all cards of that rank. A successful request earns another request; you can change ranks or opponents.
  • Drawing: after “Go Fish!”, take one card. Only drawing the requested rank gives you another request. Other drawn cards stay hidden from your opponents.
  • Books: four matching ranks leave the hand and appear in the player’s book collection. Suits identify the four cards; they do not affect points.
  • An empty hand: when it is your turn, draw up to five cards if any remain. This also applies when you empty your hand during an earned extra turn. With an empty deck, players with no cards sit out.
  • An empty deck: keep asking for cards. An unsuccessful request simply passes the turn, with no draw.
  • Ending: play ends once all 13 books have been made and no cards remain in play. Compare books to find the winner.

A Small Memory Tip

If Sarah asks Alex for queens, you know Sarah holds at least one queen at that moment. If you have a queen on your next turn, asking Sarah may be a good move. Watch transfers and completed books too: a clue can become outdated as cards move around the table. Follow each request and result in the Current Action area beside the deck. Go Fish FAQ

Can I play Go Fish online for free?

Yes. PuzzleTen’s Go Fish is free to play in your browser, with no account, registration, or download.

Can I play Go Fish against the computer?

Yes. Sarah and Alex are computer opponents. They follow the same rules as you and use public requests as clues, without seeing your hidden cards. This version does not offer online multiplayer.

How many cards do you start with in Go Fish?

In this three-player game, everyone starts with five cards. The remaining cards stay in the draw pile. Two-player Go Fish commonly starts with seven cards each.

What happens when someone says Go Fish?

It means they have none of the rank you asked for. Draw a card if the deck has any left. If it matches your request, ask again; otherwise your turn ends.

How do you win Go Fish?

Collect more books of four matching ranks than either opponent. Having the most cards in your hand does not win the game; only completed books count.

Is Go Fish a kids’ card game?

Its simple matching rules make it a familiar family game for children and adults. Younger players may enjoy help spotting matching ranks and remembering requests.

Can two people play Go Fish?

Yes, with a physical deck, Go Fish can be played by two people. This online version is set up for three players: you and two computer opponents.

Will my game continue after I close the page?

This version keeps your game while the page is open. Reloading or leaving starts a fresh game. Your browser remembers when you finish or skip the tutorial, when local storage is available.
