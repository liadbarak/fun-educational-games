FUN EDUCATIONAL GAMES

Classic games with a little learning folded in — free, instant, and playable on any phone or laptop. No download, no signup, no ads shouting over the game. Every game says right up front what it helps practise.

🔟

Make Ten

➕ Number Bonds to 10

Pairs of numbered tiles drop down. Wherever two touching tiles add up to ten, both vanish — and whatever falls into the gap can set off another match.

For parents Exercises instant recall of number bonds to 10. Spotting the pairs is the only way to play, so it's practised rather than tested.
Ages 6+ · also builds planning ahead 🍎

Food Rescue

🍎 Food Waste & Sorting

Food has been left all over the kitchen. Pick it up and put it away — anything still good goes in the fridge, anything that's just scraps goes in the bin.

For parents Exercises telling food that's still good from food that's finished with. The child carries each item to the fridge or the bin, so the decision is the move rather than an answer to a question.
Ages 5+ · also builds route planning

Every game can have its learning questions switched off, for when someone just wants to play.
