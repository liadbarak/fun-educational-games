FUN EDUCATIONAL GAMES

Classic games with a little learning folded in — free, instant, and playable on any phone or laptop. No download, no signup, no ads shouting over the game. Every game says right up front what it helps practise.

🧱

Maths Tetris

➕ Addition & Subtraction

Stack the falling blocks to clear lines. Every block carries a number, and when one lands on another they set a quick sum.

For parents Exercises mental addition and subtraction up to 18, in short bursts between rounds. A wrong answer costs nothing and shows the right one.
Ages 6+ · also builds spatial thinking ♻️

Clean-Up Snake

♻️ Recycling & Sorting

The snake is on litter patrol. Collect what people dropped in the park, and work out which bin each thing belongs in.

For parents Exercises sorting waste into recycling, compost and general rubbish. Every wrong answer explains why, so it teaches the reasoning behind the rule rather than the rule alone.
Ages 5+ · also builds planning ahead

Every game can have its learning questions switched off, for when someone just wants to play.
